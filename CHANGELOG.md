# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and this project will follow
[Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html) from 1.0 onward. Before 1.0, minor versions may include breaking changes.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Orders list: one search and filter field with an add-filter menu (payment, fulfillment, delivery status, channel, batch, tags, delivery method, PO number, dates, test orders), an "All locations" filter, a column chooser (fulfill by, channel, delivery status and method, location, batch, PO number), a warning icon on orders with address problems, an items popover per row and a figures strip (orders, items, sales, returns, fulfilled, delivered, fulfillment time) with sparklines and a comparison with the previous period (`shopmeOrdersStats`).
- Orders bulk bar: select-all-on-page menu, "show only selected", Print (order pages, packing slips, invoices for several orders), Mark as (unfulfilled, in progress, fulfilled, on hold, delivered; `shopmeOrdersMarkAs`), Capture payments (`shopmeOrdersCapture`) and fulfillment batches (`shopmeOrderBatchCreate`, `shopmeOrdersMoveToBatch`, `shopmeOrderBatchRename/Delete`, search term `batch_id:`). Orders also gain `IN_PROGRESS` as a stored fulfillment state, search terms `location_id:`, `channel:`, `delivery_status:`, `delivery_method:`, `po_number:` and `fulfill_by:`, and a fulfill-by date and PO number editable on the order page.
- Order page: test order banner, "Review address problems" banner (`shopmeAddressProblems`: missing parts, postal code format per country, phone), conversion summary (first visit, landing page, referrer and `utm_*` tags from the first-party visit data; `shopmeConversion`), a simple order risk read from the shop's own data (`shopmeRisk`), a location chip on the fulfillment and refund cards, a capture button for authorized payments, and the confirmation number as a timeline entry.
- Order timeline: attachments on comments (paperclip, `orderTimelineCommentCreate(attachments:)`), "Show email" for the emails sent to the customer (the rendered email is kept with the event) and payment details that expand under the payment entries.
- Refund page: the refund amount can be typed (plain refunds to the original payment). Order editing: custom items, a custom order-level discount and an extra shipping fee (`orderEditAddCustomItem`, `orderEditAddShippingLine`, `shopmeOrderEditSetOrderDiscount`).
- Draft orders: list with status views, filters, search by PO number and a column chooser; purchase order number; market with a quoted total in the market currency (the order itself is created in the shop currency); an estimated tax row; create a customer without leaving the draft; the draft saves itself.
- Admin: Orders > Shipping labels (orders awaiting a label, labels and voided labels; record a label with carrier, service, tracking and cost, which fulfills the order with that tracking; print a label sheet; `shopmeShippingLabelCreate/Void`). ShopMe records labels bought from a carrier and does not buy postage.
- Admin: Marketing (campaigns tied to `utm_*` tags with a link builder, budget and the orders and sales credited to each; attribution report by source, medium and campaign; `shopmeMarketingCampaign*`, `shopmeAttribution`) and a marketing card on Home. The storefront now keeps `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign` with each visit and the visit session on the order.
- Admin: Sales channels page (online store catalog with a publish-all switch, draft orders and manual sources with their orders and sales) linked from the "Sales channels" label.
- Admin: the Home "Launch your online store" button removes the storefront password after a confirmation; index pages show a back arrow and their section icon; "Learn more about ..." links under index and settings pages; the product picker searches by title, SKU or vendor with vendor, type and tag filters and warns about low stock and variants already in the order; the global search has a filter button to choose the result types; the product Share menu is disabled while there are unsaved changes.
- Sales channels: a publication per channel (the built-in Online Store, room for more) for products and collections, with `publications`, `Product/Collection.resourcePublicationsV2`, `publishablePublish` with a publish date to schedule and `publishableUnpublish`. A product that is not published on the Online Store, or whose date has not come, is no longer visible on the storefront, in search, sitemaps or the cart; collections follow the same rule.
- Products can have the `UNLISTED` status: reachable by link, absent from collections, search and sitemaps.
- Admin products: Sales channels and Catalogs rows on the product page (publish, unpublish and schedule per channel, catalog membership), a Channels popover and Catalogs column in the list, new filters (status, category, sales channel, catalog, collection), bulk Unpublish, include/exclude sales channels and catalogs, preview icon on row hover, the export dialog offers the matching search and help links.
- Admin product page: Type and Vendor comboboxes of existing values, new products default to Active, the breadcrumb shows the selected variant, metafields as one row per definition with Add chips and Show all (also before the first save, saved with the product), Duplicate dialog with inventory, translations, SKU and Unlisted options, per-variant stock locations, Available/On hand state, Unavailable and Committed read-outs with a breakdown popover, inventory history popover, package choice and customs codes by destination country, video and 3D model upload, and an image editor (crop, rotate, flip, resize, draw, background colour).
- Admin collections: sales channels column and card, image tile beside the title, a "..." menu on the type card, products filter, sort control and list/grid view of the products.
- Admin inventory: help tooltips on the columns, an adjust popover with "Adjust by", "Set to" and a recorded reason, and the unavailable/incoming breakdown.
- Discounts: minimum requirement as radios, combinations edited in a dialog, sales channel access (`shopmePublicationIds`; the storefront checkout only applies discounts that include the Online Store); gift cards list gets a column chooser and a note icon.
- Markets: a summary side panel with a toggle, a graph view of the market tree, a Rollouts page to plan when draft markets go live (`shopmeMarketLaunchSchedule`, due markets launch by themselves), the shipping options a market's countries get, and a "+" on every inherited customization.
- Catalog membership of products (`shopmeCatalogProductsUpdate`, `catalog_id:` search), per country Harmonized System codes and a shipping package on inventory items, `InventoryItem.shopmeHistory`, `productDuplicate` options `includeTranslations`, `shopmeIncludeInventory`, `shopmeIncludeSkus`.
- Theme code editor (theme "..." menu > Edit code, and Edit code in a section's menu): the theme's files in a searchable tree, a CodeMirror editor with Liquid, JSON, CSS and JavaScript highlighting, live syntax check with underlined problems, tabs with unsaved markers, add, rename and delete files, per-file version history with restore, and deleted files that can be brought back. `themeFilesUpsert` now refuses Liquid with a syntax error; new Admin API `themeFilesLint`, `themeFileVersions`, `themeFileVersion`, `themeFilesWithHistory` (migration 0018).
- Edit default theme content: every storefront text of the theme's locale files in one searchable table per language, with placeholder checks, add-a-language and unsaved-changes protection.
- Theme editor: preview data (product, collection, page, article, language and country shown in the preview), View documentation link, a live preview that is never empty in the Add section picker, section link with "open in new tab", segmented layout-direction controls, `visible_if` conditions, resource pickers for collection, product, page and blog settings, video settings with upload, "Free images" dialog, dynamic source buttons on text settings, clearable colors.
- Aurora theme: slideshow, scrolling banner, big logo, split showcase, collection links, collection list layouts (grid, carousel, bento, editorial), featured product, multicolumn, image gallery, video, testimonials, collapsible content, blog posts carousel, contact form, newsletter sign-up, custom Liquid, divider, popup, footer utilities and overlay section groups; hero and image-with-text with video media, mobile image and stacking options, section link, vertical alignment and layout direction.

Blocks gain layout, typography, color scheme, text color, background and spacing controls.

- Payments: capture method (automatic or authorise now and capture from the order, including Stripe `capture_method=manual` and the `payment_intent.amount_capturable_updated` webhook), a test gateway (sandbox payment method that marks orders as tests), merchant-defined manual payment methods with instructions, redirect payment gateways (buyer sent to a payment page URL template, confirmed by an HMAC-signed `POST /webhooks/payments/:gatewayId`), payment customizations (hide, rename or move methods by country and order total), accepted card brands shown at checkout, and a default expiration for gift cards.
- Shipping: transit time on rates (shown as an estimated delivery date at checkout, plus a store-wide handling time), rates limited to collections and to ship-from locations, carrier-calculated rates through a provider interface (demo flat provider and a generic HTTP carrier account with fixed-price fallback), order routing rules (preferred fulfilment locations by destination), delivery customizations (hide, rename, move options), fulfilment services that receive a fulfilment request email, a shipping-by-market overview, pickup of items held in other locations, local delivery follow-up (mark delivered or missed, buyer emailed), and printable invoice, packing slip and pick list templates.
- Taxes: tax region list with search and sort and a region detail page, one-click presets (EU VAT, US state sales tax, Canadian GST/PST/HST, other countries), VAT on digital goods by buyer country (EU one-stop shop), import duties collected at checkout per destination country, and customs defaults (origin country, HS code) applied to products that have none.
- Notifications: pickup (ready, picked up), local delivery (out for delivery, delivered, missed), gift card and store credit groups, fulfilment request email, and SMS templates per notification sent through a merchant-configured HTTP SMS gateway.
- Policies: generate the written policies from templates filled with the store details, multiple return rule sets per collection (window, return shipping, restocking fee) applied when a return is created.
- Languages: a domain per language (the storefront switches language by host), language suggestions from the countries of the markets, and a row menu on the default language.
- Checkout: address autofill switch and a checkout look editor (accent and background colours, logo, button shape, header and footer text) applied to the storefront checkout.
- Metafield definitions for draft orders, locations, transfers, pages, blogs, blog posts, markets and the shop, an information popover on the custom data page, and a column chooser on the locations list.
- Customers: merge two customers (`customerMerge`, preview with `shopmeCustomerMergePreview`) moving orders, drafts, addresses, tags, notes, store credit, gift cards, company links and timeline; bulk edit of tags, email and SMS consent, tax exemption and notes (`shopmeCustomerBulkUpdate`); data request export (`shopmeCustomerDataRequest`) and data erasure with a 10 day delay that anonymizes the customer and keeps the orders (`customerRequestDataErasure`, `customerCancelDataErasure`, hourly job).
- Customers admin: column chooser and column help, a query bar above the list (plain `field:value` syntax, validated, saved as a segment), "Edit in bulk", "Merge customers", "Add to company", the customer group tile, last order items, SMS consent and a dial-code select on the customer form, file attachments and "Show email" on the timeline, and the built-in segment of customers with an abandoned checkout in the last 30 days (`abandoned_checkout_in_last:30d`).

Admin API: `inventoryTransfer(s)`, `inventoryShipment(s)` and the `inventoryTransfer*` and `inventoryShipment*` mutations with typed user errors.

Read API `inventoryPurchaseOrder(s)`; writes under `shopmePurchaseOrder*` and `shopmeSupplier*`.

- Checkout configurations: Settings > Checkout lists several configurations with the active one; duplicate, rename, edit (contact and sign-in rules, address fields, marketing, tipping, branding), set active and delete (`shopmeCheckoutConfigurations`, `shopmeCheckoutConfiguration{Create,Rename,Update,Activate,Delete}`). The storefront checkout uses the active configuration.
- Notes on draft orders, customers and gift cards are collapsed behind a "+" (a pencil once there is a note) that opens an edit dialog, like the order page.
- Admin collections: the template ("Default collection") link sits beside the title, metafields are a dashed panel in the side column (also while creating, saved with the collection), and the products card gets a status filter chip with "Clear all" and a column count control for the grid view.
- Image focal point: click the image in the product media viewer to set it (`fileUpdate(files: [{ focalPoint }])`, `Image.shopmePresentation.focalPoint`, stored on the media). The storefront exposes it as `image.presentation.focal_point` and Aurora applies it as `object-position` on product cards, the product page, collection tiles and links, the featured product, hero, slideshow and the other cropped images.
- Customers: WhatsApp marketing consent next to SMS consent (checkbox on the customer form, state on the customer page, `whatsapp_subscription:` search, bulk edit, merge and the "Accepts WhatsApp Marketing" column of the CSV export; `Customer.shopmeWhatsappMarketingConsent`). Consent is only stored and exported: nothing is sent through WhatsApp.
- Per-variant Online Store availability: a variant can be hidden from the Online Store while its product stays published (`ProductVariant.shopmeHiddenFromOnlineStore`). The product page no longer offers it, and the cart refuses it. At least one variant must stay available.

### Changed

- `publishablePublish` and `publishableUnpublish` now act on the channel only: they no longer change the product status (a product is visible when it is active and published on the Online Store). Unpublished collections are no longer shown on the storefront.
- Existing shipping zones and rates move into the general profile with the same ids when the database is migrated, so carts and orders keep pointing at the right rate; the `shippingZones` and `shippingRate*` Admin API fields keep working as a view of the general profile. Shipping options at checkout are now listed cheapest first.

### Fixed

- Creating or editing a customer from the admin failed because the email marketing consent field was rejected by the API.
- `pnpm seed` now fills the default main and footer menus, which the setup step creates empty.
- Aurora: the newsletter form in the footer no longer overflows the screen on phones.
- Aurora: the footer stays at the bottom of the window on short pages (login, cart, 404).
- New stores start with Home and Catalog links in the main menu and a Search link in the footer menu, instead of empty menus.
